# Executive Memo — Customer Churn (Sample Data Only)

**Sample only — this memo is based on the synthetic sample in this repo
(1,000 customers, data/sample_customers.csv). It does NOT reproduce
the original project's figures, and the original +8% retention
projection is NOT claimed here.**

## Sample findings

- Overall sample churn: 33.9% (339 / 1,000 customers in the sample run).
- Early Tenure customers churn most in the sample (47.9%), followed by
  Mid Tenure - Standard (33.6%), Mid Tenure - Premium (30.4%), and
  Long Tenure (25.2%).
- Month-to-month contracts churn most in the sample (41.0%), vs
  One year (30.4%) and Two year (17.1%).
- Sample-only at-risk revenue: $292,202.16
  (sum of monthly_charges × 12 for churned sample customers).

## Recommended retention actions (for the sample patterns)

1. **Strengthen early-tenure onboarding** — target customers in their
   first 12 months with guided setup, check-ins, and early support,
   since this group churns most in the sample.
2. **Incentivise longer contracts** — offer month-to-month customers
   a clear reason to move to a one- or two-year plan, since longer
   contracts churn far less in the sample.
3. **Targeted save offers for at-risk segments** — prioritise
   Mid Tenure - Standard and high monthly-charge customers who churn,
   with personalised retention offers, rather than broad discounts.

## Next step

Validate these actions on real customer data before acting on them.
